\begin{abstract}


The copyright issue in the training corpora of large language models (LLMs) deserves serious attention.
Respecting copyright and intellectual property is essential, not only as a legal obligation, but also as a means of fostering a supportive and respectful environment for content creators.
Particularly, we focus our attention on copyright (or copyleft) protection of code used during the pre-training process of LLMs.


we investigates the ownership of the copyright. 
    
\end{abstract}
