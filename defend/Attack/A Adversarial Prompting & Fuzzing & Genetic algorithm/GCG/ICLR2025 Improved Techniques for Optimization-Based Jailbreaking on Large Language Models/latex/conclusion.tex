\section{Conclusion}
In this paper, we propose several improved techniques for optimization-based jaibreaking on large language models. We propose using diverse target templates, including harmful guidance, to enhance jailbreak performance. From an optimization perspective, we introduce an automatic multi-coordinate updating strategy that adaptively decides how many tokens to replace in each step. We also incorporate an easy-to-hard initialization technique, further boosting jailbreak performance. Then we combine the above improvements to develop an efficient jailbreak method, dubbed $\mathcal{I}$-GCG. Extensive experiments are conducted on various benchmarks to demonstrate the superiority of our $\mathcal{I}$-GCG.

To improve the efficiency of jailbreak, we propose an easy-to-hard optimization strategy, which first generate the jailbreak suffix on the malicious questions that easily bypass the safeguards and then adopt the generated suffix as initialization to generate the jailbreak suffix on the malicious questions that hardly bypass the safeguards. Besides, we also propose an automatic multiple coordinate update strategy to further improve jailbreak efficiency. Experimental evaluations on series of benchmark databases demonstrate that the proposed method outperforms state-of-the-art jailbreak attack methods, achieving nearly 100\% attack success rate. Moreover, the proposed method also achieves nearly 100\% success rate on the NeurIPS 2023 Red Teaming Track.
